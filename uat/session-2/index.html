13:45 to 14:20 · 35 minutes · everyone tests

Create users and give them roles

What you do

  1. In Keycloak, create a new user.
  2. Open Role mapping for that user.
  3. Assign a role, for example Applicant, Officer or Supervisor.
  4. Open the Events tab and find the change.
  5. Sign in to COMIS as that user.

What you should see

The roles update immediately and the change is logged.

The user sees the dashboard and menus of the role they were given.

14:20 to 14:30 · 10 minutes · everyone tests

Remove a role

What you do

  1. Take the user you just created.
  2. In Role mapping, remove all the roles.
  3. Open the Events tab and find the change.
  4. Sign in to COMIS as that user.

What you should see

The roles are removed and the change is logged.

The user sees an empty dashboard with no menus and a message that no roles are assigned.

14:30 to 14:50 · 20 minutes · everyone tests

Deactivate and reactivate a user

What you do

  1. Make sure the user is active, then sign in.
  2. Deactivate the user account.
  3. Try to sign in as that user.
  4. Check the user’s mailbox.
  5. Reactivate the account and sign in again.
  6. Check the change log.

What you should see

A deactivated account shows as Inactive and sign-in is refused, with a message that the account is deactivated.

No email is sent to the user. After reactivation, sign-in works again.
