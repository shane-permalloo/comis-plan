Each session is one timeline, from the start of the session to its end. Sessions 1 and 2 (Monday) are the IT administrator day; Sessions 3 to 10 are for the cooperative society, Officer and Supervisor testers, with the IT Administrator on call. MNS prepares the test data beforehand.

The four systems testers will use

Keycloak

Sign-in, password reset and user management for every COMIS user.

Public portal

WordPress site with information, publications and resources, the online search of cooperative societies and each cooperative's basic public profile.

COMIS back-office

Where cooperative societies and the Registrar's Office carry out most cooperative management, from assessment to certificate.

COMIS ticketing system

Grievance redress and the dissolution process.
