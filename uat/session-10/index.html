Before we start

Who does what, and what is ready

Who takes part

  • Cooperative Society (Applicant): requests the dissolution and checks the read-only view.
  • Registrar Officer: starts a dissolution and handles tickets.
  • Registrar Supervisor: takes the final decisions.
  • IT Administrator: on call for access problems.
  • UAT Lead and Registrar or delegate: join the closing from 16:05.

Officer handles a grievance

StepWhat to doWhat you should see
1Open a grievance and set Under Investigation.The status changes.
2Add remarks, upload an internal analysis if you wish, set Escalated to Supervisor.Officers and supervisors get an email. The sender does not.
3When sent back, upload the formal investigation report and escalate again.The report is required. Status returns to Escalated to Supervisor.
4Record the Ministry’s decision, then the Court’s judgement.Status: Ministry Decision Received, then Court Judgement Received. Grievance closed.

Catch-up

What you do

  1. Pick a test that was blocked or not finished.
  2. Run it again with the steps on the earlier slides.
  3. Record any problem you find.
  4. Ask the MNS team if you are still blocked.

The outcome

Every test is either finished or has a recorded reason why it was not.

Nothing is fixed today: problems are only recorded.
