Before we start

Who does what, and what is ready

Who does what

Cooperative Society (Applicant): elects the leaders, records meetings and changes committees.

Registrar Officer and Supervisor: open the submitted election and the meeting history. They only look: nothing can be changed.

IT Administrator: on call if an account or an email causes trouble.

What MNS has prepared

Several cooperatives with at least 20 active members, one of them inactive. An election can be submitted only once for each.

One cooperative with 12 or more submitted meetings over two years.

Cooperatives for the changes: a submitted election, an ended term, a member on several committees.

Please write down the committee names before you start the election. Still being built: results may differ.

First election of leaders

StepWhat to doWhat you should see
1Open Cooperative Profile, then Elect Leaders, to start a new election.The election page opens with AGM Details and four committee sections.

4In Executive Committee, assign Chairman, Vice Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer.The positions are already there and the committee status updates.

Find members while choosing leaders

StepWhat to doWhat you should see
1Click into the Elected Member field on an open position.A searchable dropdown opens.

Done when: picking a member puts the full name in the field and closes the list.
