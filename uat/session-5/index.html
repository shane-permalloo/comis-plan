Who is here and what is ready

Roles and prepared data

Who does what

Cooperative Society (Applicant): registers the cooperative, then views and edits its profile.

Registrar Officer: finds and reviews applications and recommends them.

Registrar Supervisor: approves, asks for changes or rejects.

IT Administrator: on call.

What MNS has prepared

Registration starts with the cooperatives that have a valid CME.

A few registrations are already submitted, so the officer can start reviewing while the applicant fills in the form.

An existing cooperative is ready for the fast track.
