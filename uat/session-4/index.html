Who does what

Three roles, one chain

Who is here

  • Cooperative society (Applicant): follows the assessment and requests CME training.
  • Registrar Officer: decides the assessment and handles the CME requests.
  • Registrar Supervisor: checks the lists, dashboards and approved requests.
  • IT Administrator: on call for accounts and access.

Prepared by MNS

Each cooperative approved in the assessment carries on into CME training, then Registration tomorrow morning.

MNS keeps a rejected assessment and an expired membership ready for the dashboard checks on Wednesday.
