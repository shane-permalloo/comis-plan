Who is here and what is ready

Roles and prepared data

Who does what

Cooperative Society (Applicant): requests training, starts the Union assessment, registers, amends and manages members.

Registrar Officer: keeps the training list, processes requests, reviews and recommends.

Registrar Supervisor: approves registrations and amendments.

IT Administrator: on call.

Prepared by MNS

Active primary cooperatives for the Union.

An approved training record for the Union.

An active post-training in the list.

Each Union step gets only 8 to 15 minutes, so this data must be ready before we start.

08:30 to 08:38 · 8 minutes

Registrar Officer

Maintain the list of post-trainings

StepWhat to doWhat you should see
1Open System Config > System References, search POST_TRAININGS and click Search.POST_TRAININGS (Post Trainings, Active) is listed.
2Click the Edit icon in the Action column.The reference opens with its list of values.
3Click +Add new value, complete the pop-up and click Create.The new value is added to the list of values.
4Edit a value: change its description or status, then click Update.The change is saved and shown in the list.

Done when: the new or edited training is saved. Only active trainings are offered to the cooperative.

Mark a request In Progress

StepWhat to doWhat you should see
1Open Applications > Post Trainings and click the Edit icon on a Pending request.The Review Post Training form opens with Action Required at top right.

Put a request On Hold

StepWhat to doWhat you should see
1Open a Pending or In Progress request with the Edit icon. Switch the On Hold toggle on.An Application on hold pop-up asks for a reason.
